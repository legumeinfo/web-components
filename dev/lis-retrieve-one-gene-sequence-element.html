<lis-retrieve-one-gene-sequence-element>

Component 1 of the retrieve-sequence spec: fetch protein / CDS / genomic sequence for a single gene by ID. The element talks to two microservices:

  • dscensor (default http://localhost:8765) resolves the gene's annotation prefix to its canonical file URLs.
  • ds_utilities (default http://localhost:8080) reads the BED to map the gene to its longest mRNA, then slices the protein / CDS / genome FASTA.
